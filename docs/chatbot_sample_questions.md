## Definitions / about RDPR

### 1. What is DCB?

**DCB**: Demand, Collection and Balance register: for each GP and year, the tax and fee demand (arrears plus the year's demand), what was collected, adjustments, and the balance still to be collected.

### 2. What is RDPR?

**The Rural Development and Panchayat Raj Department (RDPR) is the Government of Karnataka department that looks
after rural development and the administration of the Panchayat Raj institutions in the state.**

It works through the three-tier Panchayat Raj system: Zilla Panchayats at the district level, Taluk Panchayats at
the taluk level and Gram Panchayats at the village level. The department's stated vision is the comprehensive
development of rural areas through strong Panchayat Raj institutions.

Its work covers rural employment (such as MGNREGS), rural drinking water and sanitation, rural connectivity,
village development programmes, and the digital systems that Gram Panchayats use for accounts, property tax and
staff records.

### 3. What is the three-tier Panchayat Raj system?

**Karnataka has a three-tier Panchayat Raj system: Gram Panchayats at the village level, Taluk Panchayats at the
taluk level and Zilla Panchayats at the district level.**

The system was set up under the Karnataka Panchayat Raj Act, 1993. All three tiers are elected bodies; the Zilla
Panchayat is the apex institution at the district level. The Rural Development and Panchayat Raj Department
supports and supervises them.

### 4. Tell me about Panchatantra 2.0

**Panchatantra 2.0 is the RDPR Department's digital platform for Gram Panchayats: one system for accounts, property
tax, staff records, citizen services and meetings.**

It automates the accounting of GP schemes, receipts, expenditure and vouchers, digitises property tax collection,
offers citizen services such as property-related permissions, building and trade licences and online complaints,
and includes meeting management, HR and profile modules. It serves about 6,000 Gram Panchayats and was launched in
2023 as the successor of the earlier Panchatantra software. The Finance & Accounting, Revenue and HRMS figures in
this assistant mirror that portal.

**I'm the Panchayat Raj data assistant.** I answer questions about Gram Panchayat finance, revenue and staff data for Karnataka's 31 zillas, 238 taluks and 6,099 GPs from April 2022 to September 2026: book closure and scheme accounts (Finance & Accounting), demand, collection, arrears and balance by tax and property type (Revenue), and staff, EO approvals, elected members and salary bills (HRMS), by zilla, taluk, GP, financial year or month.
